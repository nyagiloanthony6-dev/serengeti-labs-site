Shehena Cargo

shehenacargo.co.tz

For cargo companies moving goods by truck and by air, from Kariakoo to Dodoma, Mwanza, Mbeya, Arusha and the rest of the country. Record every load, know which truck or flight it's on, and keep senders and receivers informed.

  • Consignments recorded with sender, receiver and destination
  • Road and air cargo managed in one system
  • Loads grouped by truck, flight and route
  • Customers told when goods leave and when they arrive

Sipiki

A multivendor marketplace where Tanzanian sellers open a shop and get paid by M-Pesa, with money held in escrow until delivery.

  • Vendor sign-up with admin approval
  • M-Pesa checkout with escrow
  • Order updates on WhatsApp

WMS · Warehouse Management System

Gives every package a place when the container is offloaded, across several warehouses and the yard, then tracks it to the gate.

  • Packing lists imported from Excel
  • Scan-driven offloading and QR location labels
  • Scan-to-move, Locator role, gate release on Delivery Orders
  • Aging and delivery dashboards; works offline

FreightHub

Tracks LCL consignments and full FCL containers from loading until the customer signs for them, and shows where shipments are getting stuck.

  • LCL and FCL shipments in one place
  • Loaded → Arrived → Cleared → Delivered
  • Customs dwell time and aging reports
  • Customer and CBM trends to spot slowing accounts
